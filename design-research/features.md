## Fit and priority
High: touch movement, guide/controls, next objective, readable party and battle health, collection search/filter and no-results recovery, accessible overlays, labeled generation form. These support the existing main action and data.
Medium: clearer existing evolution/capture/type information, loading cancellation and draft-preserving retry, shop feedback.
Deferred/needs approval: accounts/cloud save, multiplayer/trading/leaderboards, paid generation expansion, new regions/routes, backend changes. Large features seen in category leaders are not automatically appropriate to this small browser RPG.
