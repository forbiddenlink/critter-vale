Gallery provenance is recorded separately in `source-index.json`, source text files, and linked below. Current live versions may differ from historical gallery versions. This is a study of layout/material/type and usability, not permission to reuse content or assets.

Primary synthesis: Le Puzz + Nossara + Exemplar + Read-Only Memory for the journal material and hierarchy; Bruno for direct play; Interface In Game for command grouping. The plan adapts principles to the existing original creatures and mechanics.
